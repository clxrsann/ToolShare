ToolShare Rental Rules

1. Proper Use

The rented equipment must only be used for its intended purpose and according to the manufacturer's safety instructions.

2. Damage

The renter is responsible for damage caused by misuse, negligence, accidents, or unauthorized modifications.

Damage penalty may be up to the repair cost or the equipment's replacement value depending on the severity.
3. Lost Equipment

If the equipment is lost or cannot be returned, the renter may be charged the replacement value of the equipment.

Lost equipment penalty: Replacement Cost
4. Late Return

Equipment must be returned on or before the agreed return date and time.

Late return penalty: ₱100 per hour after the agreed return time.
5. Non-Return

Failure to return the equipment without notifying ToolShare may result in additional charges and account restrictions.

6. Identity Verification

ToolShare may require identity verification before releasing rented equipment.

7. Security Deposit

Some high-value equipment may require a refundable security deposit.
